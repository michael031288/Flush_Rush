# GAME SPECIFICATION: Flush Rush

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Game Title:** Flush Rush
- **Protagonist:** Strawberry (a chubby, cute white chibi bunny with pink accents)
- **Target Audience:** 7-year-old child (forgiving controls, clear visual feedback, no permadeath).
- **Engine / Tech Stack:** Godot 4.x (GDScript)
- **Genre:** 2D Side-Scrolling Action / Water Platformer
- **Visual Style:** 2D Cute Anime Pixel Art set against a moody, slightly spooky sewer (pipes, glowing green slime, floating crates, glowing mushrooms).
- **Core Loop:** Swim through sewer water, leap out onto floating pipes and platforms, smash mystery boxes for power-ups, dodge sewer monsters, defeat the area boss, and return home to Strawberry's bunny family.

---

## 2. Narrative & World Premise

### 2.1 Intro Sequence (`IntroCutscene.tscn`)
- A lightweight 2D vignette using 3-4 slides or an animated sequence:
  1. Strawberry the bunny hops around near a bathroom toilet chasing a sparkly butterfly.
  2. Comical *whoosh!* — Strawberry slips into the bowl and gets flushed with a swirl effect.
  3. Strawberry shoots out of a large rusty sewer pipe into murky water below.

### 2.2 Tone & Atmosphere
- Spooky and mysterious sewer ambient lighting, dripping water sound effects, and dark teal/purple palettes, contrasted with Strawberry's bright white-and-pink character.

### 2.3 Victory Condition (`EndingCutscene.tscn`)
- Strawberry climbs a glowing ladder out of a manhole into a sunny meadow.
- Strawberry's bunny family is waiting with celebratory music, party confetti, and fireworks particles (`GPUParticles2D`).

---

## 3. Core Mechanics & Godot Architecture

### 3.1 Strawberry (Player Node: `CharacterBody2D`)
- **State Machine:**
  - `SWIMMING` (Inside `WaterZone` Area2D):
    - Free 8-directional movement using `Input.get_vector("ui_left", "ui_right", "ui_up", "ui_down")`.
    - Reduced gravity, slight water drag/damping, upward buoyancy drift when idle.
  - `AIRBORNE` / `LAND`:
    - Triggered by jumping while near the water surface or landing on dry platforms (`TileMapLayer` or `StaticBody2D`).
    - Standard 2D platformer gravity and jumping.
- **Health & Power-down Rule:**
  - Max Health: 3 Carrots.
  - **Power-up Buffer:** If Strawberry touches an enemy while holding an active power-up, Strawberry drops the power-up immediately instead of losing a Carrot.
  - **Hurt State:** 1.5s invulnerability window with sprite flashing (`modulate.a` flickering).
  - **Defeat:** At 0 Carrots, Strawberry floats back to the last pipe checkpoint (`Marker2D`).

---

## 4. Power-Ups & Mystery Boxes

### 4.1 Mystery Box (`Area2D`)
- Floating crates marked with a glowing `?`.
- On player body enter: Trigger bounce animation, play sound, break open, and drop 1 random power-up collectible.

### 4.2 Power-Up Types & Effects

| Power-Up | Godot Implementation | Visual Indicator | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Speed Boost** | Multiplies `speed` and `jump_velocity` by `1.8x` | Trail particles (`CPUParticles2D`) | 8 seconds |
| **Invincibility** | Temporarily toggles collision mask/layer; destroys enemies on contact | Rainbow shader or rapid color flashing | 10 seconds |
| **Invisibility** | Enemy detection areas ignore player; passes through hazards safely | Set sprite `modulate.a = 0.35` (ghostly see-through) | 10 seconds |

---

## 5. Enemies & Level Boss

### 5.1 Regular Hazards
- **Sewer Slimes (`Area2D`):** Toxic hazards sticking to pipe walls and floors.
- **Sewer Eels (`CharacterBody2D`):** Patrol vertically in water tunnels.
- **Cave Bats (`CharacterBody2D`):** Fly horizontally across the ceiling dry sections.

### 5.2 Level 1 Boss: The Sewer King Gator (`CharacterBody2D`)
- Arena with deep water in the center and elevated dry pipe platforms on both sides.
- **Boss States:**
  1. **Submerge & Charge:** Swims rapidly across the bottom water lane (player must leap out onto high pipes to avoid).
  2. **Slime Splash:** Boss hits the water, launching falling slime projectiles (`RigidBody2D` or `Area2D`).
  3. **Vulnerable Window:** Boss pauses to catch its breath. Mystery boxes spawn into the arena; Strawberry grabs **Invincibility** or **Speed** to dash-attack the boss.
  4. Takes 3 hits to defeat.
